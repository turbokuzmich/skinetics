## Working positioning

Skinetics explains and compares products across the portfolio, helps visitors choose by concern, ingredient, product type, or brand, and then sends purchase-ready visitors to Wildberries or Ozon.

This positioning and the three-brand structure were accepted in [decision 0001](../decisions/0001-brand-architecture.md).

## Accepted rules

1. Every product belongs to exactly one customer-facing brand.
2. Use `SkineticsLab` in site text; uppercase marketplace or packaging artwork is a visual styling, not the text spelling.
3. ReneWal is a product name under SkineticsLab, not a brand.
4. Use text labels until official logo files and brand guidelines are available; do not invent permanent logos from marketplace artwork.

## Required evidence

- current packaging photography;
- manufacturer product documents and declarations where applicable;
- approved brand logos and spelling;
- current Wildberries and Ozon cards;
- written owner confirmation when marketplace data conflicts with packaging.

Official logo files and brand guidelines were unavailable as of 2026-08-22. This is recorded as a Phase 04 input, not a Phase 01 blocker.

The three-brand text treatment was applied to the Phase 02 local release candidate and validated on 2026-08-23. Production deployment remains pending.

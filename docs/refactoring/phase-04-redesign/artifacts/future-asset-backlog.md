## Intake record

For every supplied asset, record:

- source and rightsholder;
- received, creation, or issue date;
- represented company, brand, or product;
- publication and modification permission;
- expiry or next-review date;
- required attribution;
- claims or regulatory review status;
- accessible alternative text or transcript;
- approved output formats and responsive renditions;
- final repository location and consuming pages.

Do not publish an expired, unverified, mismatched, permission-unclear, or claims-sensitive asset merely because it is present in the repository.

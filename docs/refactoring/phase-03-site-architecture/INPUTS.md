## Evidence limitations and unavailable inputs

- There is no meaningful historical Skinetics traffic, numerical keyword-volume study, Yandex Webmaster export, or Google Search Console export.
- Phase 02 production deployment and its monitoring window remain pending separate authorization.
- Official brand logos and guidelines are unavailable; text labels remain accepted until approved assets exist.
- Available expert, photography, and ongoing content-production capacity has not been established.
- Marketplace questions and reviews identify customer information needs but do not verify efficacy, safety, compatibility, concentration, or product facts.
- Medical, regulatory, clinical, quantified, guaranteed-effect, safety-sensitive, minor-use, diagnosed-condition, and treatment-adjacent statements require external professional or legal review.

# Phase 05 inputs

## Business and operating inputs

- named marketing owner and contributors;
- available hours by role;
- monthly paid, content, creator, tooling, and contractor budget;
- target products and marketplace priorities;
- marketplace margins or contribution constraints where available;
- current campaign history and lessons.

## Audience and content inputs

- accepted customer segments and primary jobs-to-be-done;
- search-query and landing-page baseline;
- marketplace question and review-theme summaries;
- Phase 03 content map and priorities;
- approved expert, creator, photography, and production assets;
- claims review capacity.

## Measurement inputs

- verified marketplace click events;
- campaign and UTM conventions;
- traffic and conversion baselines;
- marketplace external-traffic reports where available;
- attribution limitations and reporting lag;
- agreed north-star and leading indicators.

## Key open decisions

1. What monthly marketing budget is available for the first 90 days?
2. Who owns strategy, content approval, creative production, and reporting?
3. Which marketplace should receive priority when both are available?
4. What downstream marketplace evidence is reliable enough for scale decisions?

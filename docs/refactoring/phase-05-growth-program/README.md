# Phase 05 — Growth program

- **State:** Not started
- **Owner:** Unassigned
- **Started:** —
- **Completed:** —

## Objective

Run a measurable 90-day acquisition and optimization program that increases qualified visits and marketplace clicks without turning Skinetics into a marketplace clone.

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Phase 04 complete
- [ ] Product- and marketplace-level outbound measurement verified
- [ ] Search and content baselines available
- [ ] Marketing owner, execution capacity, and budget documented
- [ ] Marketplace attribution limitations documented

## In scope

- 90-day acquisition and content roadmap;
- search, expert, comparison, selection-tool, QR, creator, and retargeting experiments;
- experiment briefs, measurement, review cadence, and backlog prioritization;
- marketplace-specific conversion analysis.

## Out of scope

- unmeasured campaign volume;
- generic mass content generation;
- medical diagnosis or personalized treatment recommendations;
- claiming sales attribution that cannot be supported.

## Deliverables

- accepted 90-day marketing plan with owners and budget;
- prioritized experiment backlog;
- first high-intent content cluster;
- campaign and marketplace attribution conventions;
- weekly and monthly reporting format;
- end-of-cycle findings and next-cycle recommendations.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] Every experiment has a hypothesis, owner, cost, audience, metric, stop rule, and review date.
- [ ] Content is prioritized from measured demand and differentiated from marketplace cards.
- [ ] Outbound clicks are segmented by source, content, product, and marketplace.
- [ ] The first 90-day cycle has documented results and decisions.
- [ ] The next backlog is prioritized from evidence.
